Zero-Trust IdP Mesh

Passwordless sign-in with a DID and a passkey (WebAuthn).

1. Your identity (DID)

The DID is created in this browser. Its private key never leaves it.

2. Register a passkey

Uses Windows Hello, a phone or a security key. Needs a PIN or biometric.
